## 3. How Information Is Used

Information collected through the Portal may be used to:

- Provide access to benefits education and resources.
- Display relevant benefits information and plan materials.
- Answer questions submitted through a benefits assistant or chat feature.
- Provide results from calculators, planners, and other interactive tools.
- Facilitate requested appointments, communications, webinars, or educational activities.
- Send reminders, announcements, and benefits communications you have requested or that relate to your benefits, such as meeting reminders or enrollment deadlines.
- Allow the Publishing Organization to follow up on requests you make, including questions about services such as financial planning, rollovers, or insurance coverage.
- Personalize content, such as showing information in your preferred language or highlighting resources relevant to your plan.
- Maintain Portal functionality and security, and prevent fraud or misuse.
- Troubleshoot technical issues and respond to support requests.
- Measure educational content engagement, produce engagement reports, and improve the user experience.
- Comply with applicable legal requirements and authorized contractual obligations.

The Portal's educational content, assistant responses, and tool results are not a determination of benefit eligibility, insurance coverage, claim approval, or investment suitability. The Portal does not make automated decisions that produce legal or similarly significant effects about you.
